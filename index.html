Your Yoga flow instructions

  1. Pick your favorite 2 Standing asanas + 2 Sitting asanas + 2 Lying down asanas.
  2. Keep this order: Standing ➡ Sitting ➡ Lying down.
  3. Perform each asana 3 times. 🔁
  4. Take 10 natural belly breathes for each lapse.
  5. Start your 6 set with Suryanamaskaram.
